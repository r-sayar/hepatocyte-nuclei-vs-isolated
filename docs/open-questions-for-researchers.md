# Open questions for the original authors

Each item: whom to ask, what exactly, and why it matters here. Find contact details on the paper's corresponding-author line; never guess an address. Drafts go to `docs/outreach/`; the repo owner sends.

1. **Andrews et al. 2024, J Hepatol (PMID 38199298), GSE247128.** Ask whether they can share the filtered cell barcodes and cell-type labels for the PSC/PBC sc and sn libraries (GEO has raw matrices only), and how ambient RNA was handled. Also ask whether bulk RNA-seq exists for any of the same explants. Why: cell calling and ambient RNA drive the hepatocyte-nucleus pseudobulk (H2), and same-explant bulk would give a paired design.
2. **Andrews et al. 2022, Hepatol Commun (PMID 34792289), GSE185477.** Ask what distinguishes the CST, NST and TST nuclei protocols, and whether the TST suspensions were filtered or sorted. Why: nucleus purity sets how much cytoplasmic and ambient RNA enters the snRNA profile.
3. **Innsbruck normothermic machine perfusion studies.** Bulk RNA-seq GSE263614 and BD Rhapsody scRNA-seq GSE216584 (verify the authors on the GEO and paper pages). Ask for the key linking scRNA livers to bulk livers. Why: same-liver, same-timepoint bulk vs sc would be the only paired human-liver design with an inflammatory swing.
4. **Gubra (2023 poster: human NASH biopsies with bulk RNA-seq and snRNA-seq from the same biopsies).** Ask whether the data can be shared or will be deposited. Why: exactly the paired design this project lacks.
5. **ENCODE4 liver (Snyder lab snRNA-seq, Wold lab total RNA-seq).** Ask whether the snRNA-seq and the total RNA-seq of a donor came from the same tissue piece. Why: it decides whether the ENCODE comparison is same-piece or same-donor.
